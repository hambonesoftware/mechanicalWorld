Source: [Innerscene — Measure Slide / Vernier Caliper](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/measure-slide-vernier-caliper-e27eece9)

Original file: `vernier-caliper.glb` (approximately 275 KB), made available by Innerscene under [Creative Commons CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The author grants unrestricted use, modification, and redistribution, including in commercial applications. This file is bundled with REDLINE for fast offline-friendly loads and is not fetched at play time. A deterministic download script runs only when the vendored binary is absent.

This is a vernier caliper source model, not a calibrated digital measuring device. REDLINE simulates the readout and may add a separate overlay. The CAD scale in the scene is illustrative, and gameplay readings are not physically traceable.
