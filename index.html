The dropped block is a separate rigid-body demonstration. It does not impose the beam's calculated tip force.

How to play

Build — tap the beam or drag handles to change its shape. The bottom editor provides precise dimensions.

Test — the hydraulic ram ramps to the target. After testing, choose Unload to reveal permanent bending, Reload for the hysteresis curve, or Curve to scrub recorded steps. Use Break It for an illustrative tearing failure.

Inspect — examine stress, elastic strain, deflection and yield safety factor. Drag the scene to orbit; pinch to zoom.

Elastic FEAScript remains the baseline. Loading/unloading uses a one-dimensional numerical fiber beam with bilinear elastoplastic material behavior and an estimated permanent set. It is a simplified small-deflection model, not validated 3D FEA. Crack growth, tearing and rupture remain illustrative, not material-calibrated predictions. After separation, the actuator command is not force carried by the beam. Never use gameplay thresholds for real structural design.
