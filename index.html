How to play

Build — tap the beam or drag handles to change its shape. The bottom editor provides precise dimensions.

Test — the hydraulic ram gradually loads the beam. It stops at the chosen target or the first-yield threshold.

Inspect — examine stress, elastic strain, deflection and yield safety factor. Drag the scene to orbit; pinch to zoom.

Linear-elastic Euler–Bernoulli calculations remain the verified baseline. After first yield, extra bending, crack growth, tearing and separation are an illustrative gameplay damage model, not validated nonlinear FEA. The displayed command after fracture is not force carried by the detached beam. Never use the visual failure threshold for structural design.
